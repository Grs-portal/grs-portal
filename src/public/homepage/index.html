Welcome To Green Recovery Space

A place to catch your breath
and find direction again

The Green Recovery Space at Hòfi Kòrsou offers a safe, natural place where people are given the space to recover, catch their breath, and find direction again. This is achieved through connecting with peers, accessible activities, and various learning opportunities.

For whom

Do you feel like you are stuck, for example, due to stress, mental overload, or loneliness? This can happen to anyone and is part of life.

Want to know more?


What do we offer?

Support from people who understand what you are going through because they have had similar experiences.

Want to know more?


What do we believe in?

We believe in a society where people facing challenges in life can access support in a low-threshold and welcoming way, enabling them to regain balance.
