Kick-off meeting Green Recovery Space on May 1

posted on: 9 march 2026

On May 1, 2026, Green Recovery Space will organize a special kick-off meeting at Hòfi Kòrsou. The afternoon offers professionals, organizations and interested parties the chance to get to know the workshop and discover what it can mean for people who are stuck.

Visitors will gain a sense of Green Recovery Space's vision and mission, as well as the unique role that peer support professionals play in the recovery process. Green Recovery Space demonstrates how it is a valuable addition to the care and support landscape for people who, for whatever reason, are temporarily or permanently stuck. Green Recovery Space does not replace regular care but can provide support before, during, or after a care pathway.

In addition, four participants completed a follow-up program to become Basic Trainer & Facilitator, which allows them to train future experts by experience and thus further professionalize the field.

Using personal stories, experts demonstrate how a place like Green Recovery Space could have helped them and how they can guide others in regaining balance, direction, and meaning. This provides a tangible picture of the impact a recovery workshop can have.

In addition to the stories, there will be opportunities to talk with the Green Recovery Space team, ask questions, and explore the Hòfi Kòrsou. Visitors will also receive information on how to get involved: by contributing ideas, making a referral, or sharing the initiative within their own networks.

With this meeting, Green Recovery Space aims to inspire and invite a broad network to actively participate in building a place where people, at their own pace, receive support, space, and confidence to shape their recovery and find meaning.
