Program & Activities

Trainings and activities for recovery, reflection, and connection.

The Green Recovery Space offers a welcoming place where people can join activities, learn about recovery, meet others, and find practical support for well-being. This page provides an overview of the trainings and activities currently offered. These may continue to grow and evolve over time.

Ask about the program

Trainings & activities

These courses and activities are designed to support recovery, self-understanding, resilience, and connection with others.

Recovery plan

WRAP

WRAP (Wellness Recovery Action Plan) is a practical, recovery-oriented approach that helps you reflect on what you need to feel well. In a small, safe group, you take time to explore what supports you in daily life – and what you can do when things become more difficult.

Read more →
Reflection

Zicht op herstel

Zicht op herstel is a recovery-oriented course for people who want to reflect on their life story and on what recovery means for them personally. It is not about solving problems or making plans, but about creating space to understand, feel, and gradually find new direction.

Read more →
Connection

JIJenIK

JIJenIK is a recovery-oriented group course in which connection is central – connection with yourself, with others, and with what happens between people. In JIJenIK, you explore how you relate to others, and what feels helpful or difficult in those connections.

Read more →
Personal story

Eigen verhaal

Eigen verhaal is a recovery-oriented course in which you create space for your personal narrative. In Eigen verhaal, you explore how you can tell your story in a way that does justice both to what you have experienced and to who you are.

Read more →
Support tool

Crisiskaart

The Crisiskaart is a personal document in which you record what is important to you in a crisis situation. It helps you clearly communicate who you are, what you need, and what others can do to support you as well as possible.

Read more →
Resilience

Rots & Water

Rots & Water is a resilience training programme that helps you develop a balance between strength and flexibility. Through physical and experiential exercises, you learn when it is important to stand firm and when it is helpful to adapt.

Read more →
