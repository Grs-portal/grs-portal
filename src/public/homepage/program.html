Program & Activities

Activities, learning, and recovery support at your own pace.

The Green Recovery Space offers a welcoming place where people can join activities, learn about recovery, meet others, and find practical support for well-being. The program is designed to be accessible, human, and connected to real lived experience.

Ask about the program Explore activities

What is this page about?

This page gives visitors a clear overview of what they can expect from the Green Recovery Space program. It explains the types of activities, the way people can participate, and how the program supports recovery, balance, and connection.

The program may grow and change over time. New activities, trainings, and schedules can be added without changing the structure of this page.

The goal is not to pressure people, but to create space where they can breathe, learn, connect, and take steps forward in their own rhythm.

Why it matters

Many people need a place where they can feel welcome without having to explain everything immediately. A recovery-oriented program gives room for personal growth, peer connection, and practical learning.

The Green Recovery Space combines activities, training, self-help, and lived-experience knowledge in a way that feels approachable and supportive.

Types of activities

The exact offer can be updated over time, but these are the main types of activities and learning opportunities that fit within the Green Recovery Space.

Recovery

Rest & reflection

Activities that help people slow down, reflect, and reconnect with themselves in a calm environment.

Learning

Self-help & recovery knowledge

Practical sessions focused on recovery, well-being, coping skills, and understanding personal processes.

Connection

Peer exchange

Spaces where people can meet others, share experiences if they want, and feel less alone.

Training

Lived-experience development

Learning opportunities connected to lived experience, recovery-oriented work, and supporting others.

How participation works

Visitors should quickly understand that participation is approachable. The program is not about pressure, performance, or doing everything at once.

Step 1

Explore what fits

People can first read about the program, ask questions, or look at the available activities before deciding what feels right.

Step 2

Join at your own pace

Participation can happen step by step. Visitors do not need to know everything in advance or commit to everything immediately.

Step 3

Grow through support

Through activities, learning, and connection, people can discover tools and support that help them move forward.
