Who is the Green Recovery Space for?

The Green Recovery Space is for anyone who needs rest, recovery, and a chance to find direction again. You do not need a label, diagnosis, or referral. You are not committing to anything.

Whether you feel stuck due to stress, loss, mental health challenges, overload, or simply feel that things are not going well right, now you are welcome to explore together what might help you.

Do you recognize yourself in this?

People come to the Green Recovery Space when they:

  • Feel overwhelmed or exhausted for a longer period of time
  • Are recovering from mental health challenges or a difficult period in life
  • Want to rebuild rhythm and structure in their daily life
  • Are looking for space for rest and reflection
  • Would like to meet others who are going through something similar
  • Want to learn how to take better care of themselves

Everyone arrives with their own story. There is no single reason required to be welcome here.

Who is it less suitable for?

The Green Recovery Space is not a crisis service, nor a detox or rehab center, and it does not replace medical or specialist care. If you are experiencing acute or intensive support needs, we are happy to think along about where appropriate support can be found at that moment.

What is important to know

    At the Green Recovery Space:
  • You do not need a diagnosis or referral
  • Participation is voluntary
  • You decide your own pace and level of involvement
  • Personal choice and self-direction are central

You are welcome to visit and explore whether it feels like the right place for you.
