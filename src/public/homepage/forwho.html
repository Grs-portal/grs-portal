Who is the Green Recovery Space for?

The Green Recovery Space is for anyone who needs rest, recovery, and a chance to find direction again. You do not need a label, diagnosis, or referral. You are not committing to anything.

Whether you feel stuck due to stress, loss, mental health challenges, overload, or simply feel that things are not going well right, now you are welcome to explore together what might help you.

Do you recognize yourself in this?

Maybe you recognize one or more of these situations:

  • Your mind never really stops
  • You feel disconnected from yourself
  • Everything takes effort, even things that used to be normal
  • You're looking for peace, stability, or meaning, but you don't know how

You do not need to explain this clearly.

At the Green Recovery Space, you can start where you are now.
