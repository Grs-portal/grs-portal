Green Recovery space

A safe place in nature to catch your breath and find direction at your own pace. Hòfi Kòrsou is more than just a place. A hòfi represents community, history, and growth. At Green Recovery Space, it's a garden where people can recover and flourish.

What it is (and what it isn't)

The Green Recovery Space is not a replacement for regular care, but a supplement to it. For many people, this is a valuable addition to therapy, guidance, or other forms of care. Sometimes before, sometimes during, and sometimes after a care program. But even without a care program, you are very welcome at Green Recovery Space.

For intensive or acute care needs, we are happy to discuss with you what is appropriate and where you can go.

What can you expect?

Peace & Security

A place where you don't have to prove anything. Plenty of space, surrounded by nature, and dedicated to your process.

Grow at your own pace

We offer support through activities, a rhythm, and attendance without obligations or performance pressure.

Meeting

You'll meet others who are also working on recovery. Meeting is free of charge: participate, listen, or simply take a moment for yourself.

Light structure

You'll meet others who are also working on recovery. Meeting is free of obligation: participate, listen, or simply take a moment for yourself.

Recovery Workshops

🌿

What is a recovery workshop?

The recovery workshop is an open, safe space where you can work on your recovery in your own way and at your own pace. It's not a care facility, but a place to relax, discover what helps you, and find your way back step by step.

🌱

Who is it for?

For anyone who needs space for recovery, balance, and connection. You don't need a diagnosis and you're not committed to anything. You're welcome just the way you are.

🛠️

What can you do there?

You can participate in activities, training sessions, or self-help methods, or just be there. Meet, learn, practice, or even do nothing. Everything is allowed, nothing is required.

🌞

What does it add beyond care?

The recovery workshop connects with daily life. It offers a place to practice what you learn, to strengthen self-management, and to shape recovery outside of the treatment setting.

How do we work?

Recovery is about finding direction in your life again, in a way that suits you. Not by reducing symptoms, but by restoring hope, personal strength, and purpose.

Within the Green Recovery Space, we work from this recovery-oriented perspective, with strong attention to the Caribbean context and the power of lived experience.

Diagnosos-Free, Voluntary and Safe
There are no labels or obligations. Participation is voluntary, and together we create a safe and respectful environment.
Education & Structured Self-Help
We offer simple, practical knowledge and methods that help you better understand what's going on within you and what you can do to enhance your well-being. Read more about our program and offerings here. View our Program & Registration
Experience-Based Expertise
Experience is just as valuable as professional knowledge for us. We work with people who use their own recovery experiences to support and guide others.
Peer Support
You'll meet others with their own experiences of recovery. Equality and recognition are key.
