What is Green Recovery Space

The Green Recovery Space is a recovery-oriented community environment located in nature, designed to support mental wellbeing, emotional healing and personal growth. It provides a non-clinical, low-threshold and nature-based setting where individuals can participate in activities that promote resilience, connection and recovery.

The Green Recovery Space focuses on:

  • Nature-based healing (walking, mindfulness, outdoor activities)
  • support and shared lived experience
  • Recovery-oriented workshops
  • safe, peaceful environment for reflection and empowerment

It was created as a supportive space where people can work on their mental health while being surrounded by nature, reducing stress and strengthening wellbeing.

Meet Our Team

Our team is made up of dedicated professionals, creatives, and community advocates who share one goal: improving mental well-being through care, education, and meaningful projects.

Each member brings unique expertise, but we are all guided by the same commitment: supporting mental health with dignity and respect.
